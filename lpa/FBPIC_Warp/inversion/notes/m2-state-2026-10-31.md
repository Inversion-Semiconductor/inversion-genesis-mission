## Findings so far

1. Tangent cost on Twin-XS: 22.3 ms per step with K = 1, against 9.2 ms for the primal step (2.4x).
2. Twin-XS particle tangents grow fast: the largest |d ux / d E_L| goes from 65 (step 10) to 8.5e3 (step 20) and 1.2e6 (step 30) per J. The particles are in the laser region at r = 1 to 3 um, not near the axis. Twin-XS resolves lambda0 with 3.85 cells (dz = 0.208 um, c dt = dz), so the discrete map may be strongly chaotic. L3 and short-horizon L4 decide whether the tangents are correct. The L4 pass horizons beyond about 20 steps are at risk.
